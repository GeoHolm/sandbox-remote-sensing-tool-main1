Nearly every sustainability claim made about cotton rests on what someone remembered and wrote down.

Self-reported practice data is costly to collect, impossible to audit at scale, and slow to arrive. Brands are being asked to verify what nobody can see.

Meanwhile satellites photograph every cotton field in the country every five days, free of charge, and have done since 2017. The question is whether that imagery can carry the claim.

We spent this year finding out. This deck is what we learned, including where it fell short.

This is the framing slide. Do not rush it. The audience knows the self-reporting problem intimately, because they pay for it. Land the last line so the honesty later reads as credibility rather than retreat.
