Cotton is the strong crop. It misses almost nothing: 97% of cover crops and 83% of conservation-tilled fields are caught.

False alarms are the weak point. The system over-calls conservation practice, which is exactly what calibration data fixes.

Cover crop and tillage thresholds fitted in-sample; cross-validated they fall to 75.2% and 73.8%.

Do not hide the footnote. If asked, the honest line is that the in-sample figures are the optimistic view and the cross-validated ones are what to plan against. Both are in the written report.
