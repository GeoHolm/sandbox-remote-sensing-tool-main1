Two cotton fields, two years, every clear day

Green is living cover. Tan is residue. White is bare soil. Both fields grow continuous cotton, so the crop is not what separates them.

The Georgia field is cover cropped and never opens up. The Texas pivot returns to bare ground after every harvest and stays there until June.

Sentinel-2, all cloud-free dates. Dashed lines mark inferred planting and harvest.
